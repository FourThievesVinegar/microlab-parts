# Alternate Control Box and Reactor Manifold Design

These files were sent to us by an independent replicator. We have not tested or vetted them, but the included media shows how it works and fits together. It is based on the v0.6.0 designs and the photos include some parts that are no longer needed, like the switchable IOT power plug.

We are including it here for posterity so that others can experiment and continue to remix the project.

## Alternate Components

- Display: [ROADOM 10.1" Touchscreen Monitor, 1024x600 IPS Raspberry Pi Screen, HDMI](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09XDK2FRR?th=1)
- Outer jar: larger 1/2 gallon mason jar

## Alternate Assembly Info

### The Control Box

The screen mounts into the case with M3 screws. All the components (arduino with pump controllers, 5v regulator and relays module) all mount to standoff posts using M3 screw so everything stays in place and doesn't use snap tabs that often break when 3D printed. The two halves of the case use 4 M4x50mm screws to hold them together; the holes for the screw heads weren't quite big enough but you can use some M4 rubber feet. The design also might need some extra vent holes if the pump controllers get too hot, possibly with a small computer fan for circulation. The design also adds strips of velcro to the 12V power supply and IOT plug (IOT plug shown in images is not needed for the 1.0 MicroLab) so everything is held together as one solid unit.

### The Manifold

The manifold design is a tweaked version of the GL45 manifold with some extra changes - it adds a flange on the edges of the manifold where it sits on the top of the jar and has a better rim that screws onto the mason jar. It also changes where the motor sits to be much more snug. The thermometer wire goes through a slanted hole right on the edge of the lid (this may require reaming the hole with a drill but it fits great). The design also makes the circulation pipe holes big enough that they can fit 5.5mm silicone grommets (8mm drill hole).
